# EduZone Learn — Prep 3 Science, Term 1 (2026–2027)

## Evidence and scope

The user's uploaded two-page contents page shows exactly four units and ten lessons, with the lesson opening page numbers 2, 15, 25, 35, 46, 54, 65, 75, 85 and 95. Its sequence matches the current Egyptian National Preparatory 3 Science "Discover and Learn" first-term contents for 2026–2027.

This is the **National** subject pathway. The **British** switch exposes the same topics only as optional cross-curricular enrichment, not an official mapping to British Year 9 or GCSE specifications. The American pathway remains unverified and intentionally does not expose it as a matched syllabus.

## Unit and lesson outline

1. Chemical Reactions and Their Environmental Impacts
   - Types of Chemical Reactions (p. 2): reaction patterns, metal reactivity series, displacement, oxidation and reduction.
   - Combustion Reactions and Environmental Pollution (p. 15): fire triangle, complete/incomplete burning, oxides, air pollution.
   - Smoking and Its Hazards (p. 25): tobacco smoke composition, direct and passive exposure, health and prevention.
2. Electricity and Magnetism
   - Electric Current (p. 35): electric charge, I = Q/t, current, voltage, cells, resistance, circuit symbols.
   - Electric Circuits (p. 46): Ohm's law, series/parallel resistors, electrical power and energy, safety.
   - Electric and Magnetic Forces (p. 54): electromagnetic field, electromagnets, motors and generators.
3. Variation of Traits in Living Organisms
   - Heredity and Variation of Traits (p. 65): DNA, genes, alleles, Mendelian inheritance and Punnett squares.
   - Natural Selection and Artificial Selection (p. 75): environmental pressure, natural selection and selected breeding.
4. Dating Planet Earth
   - The Fossil Record (p. 85): fossil formation/types, sedimentary layers, relative age and index fossils.
   - Geologic Time (p. 95): geologic time scale, superposition and relative age, radioactive dating and half-life.

## Accuracy limitations

The uploaded PDF contains the table of contents only, not the ten complete textbook lessons. The app's conceptual explanations, stories, diagrams, experiments and quiz questions are **newly written** and use the matching curriculum topic map. They are not copied text, not direct transcriptions, and are not claimed to be an exact page-by-page equivalent of the complete Ministry book. A full line-by-line syllabus audit against all textbook pages and teacher review is still required before claiming exhaustive coverage.

Narration is browser text-to-speech (female voice preferred when available). This does not guarantee female speech or exact Egyptian-dialect pronunciation on every device; no prerecorded professional voice assets are included.

The simulations are simplified instructional models. They are not physical laboratories or empirical measurements.
